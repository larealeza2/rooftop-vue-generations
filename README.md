SECTION 1: PROJECT OVERVIEW
Build a premium, visually immersive, multi-page restaurant website for Rooftop Restaurant & Lounge, located on the 6th Floor of K Hotels, Plot 32 Hill Rd, Entebbe, Uganda. This is not a standard restaurant website — it is a luxury lifestyle destination experience sitting above Lake Victoria, Uganda's most iconic body of water.
The website must communicate three core pillars simultaneously:

Fine dining prestige (multi-cuisine, themed events, private hire)
Lifestyle destination (rooftop pool, lounge, nightclub, sunset views)
Emotional escape ("home away from home," breathtaking Lake Victoria views)

The site must serve both local Ugandan diners and international hotel guests, functioning as the primary digital face of the rooftop experience and driving direct reservations.
Parent brand: K Hotels — TripAdvisor's #1 Hotel in Entebbe. The restaurant site must reflect and elevate this ranking as social proof throughout.

SECTION 3: USER PSYCHOLOGY
The website must engineer the following emotional journey from the moment the user lands:
Step 1 — Awe (0–3 seconds)
The hero section must deliver an immediate, visceral reaction. The user must feel like they are already on the rooftop. Use full-screen cinematic imagery or video of the Lake Victoria view at golden hour. No clutter. No menus blocking the view. Just the vista.
Step 2 — Desire (3–15 seconds)
Transition from awe to wanting to be there. Introduce the dining experience, cocktail culture, and rooftop lifestyle. Trigger FOMO — "Sunset Sessions," "Mongolian Night," and "Private Dining" feel like exclusive events the user shouldn't miss.
Step 3 — Trust (15–45 seconds)
Anchor credibility. TripAdvisor #1 badge. Guest reviews with real emotional quotes. Parent hotel association. Photography of real, beautiful food and real people enjoying themselves.
Step 4 — Intent (45 seconds–2 minutes)
Make action effortless. Clear menu, easy navigation, a reservation CTA that appears at every scroll point. The user should feel friction-free momentum toward booking.
Step 5 — Commitment (Reservation)
The reservation experience must feel premium, not transactional. Personalized field labels, elegant form design, confirmation language that feels like a warm welcome rather than a database entry.
Key psychological triggers to deploy:

Scarcity: "Limited rooftop tables available" or "Private dining subject to availability"
Social proof: Real reviews prominently displayed, TripAdvisor #1 badge
Exclusivity: Language like "Reserve Your Rooftop Experience" not "Book a Table"
Sensory language: Copy must invoke sight (lake views, golden hour), taste (multi-cuisine), sound (cocktail lounge, nightclub), and feel (warm staff, home away from home)
Authority: TripAdvisor ranking, K Hotels brand heritage

Alt text: Every image has descriptive, meaningful alt text (e.g., "Rooftop dining table overlooking Lake Victoria at sunset, K Hotels Entebbe").
Focus indicators: All interactive elements have visible gold focus rings.
Form labels: All form fields have explicit  elements — never rely on placeholder text alone.
Semantic HTML: Correct heading hierarchy (H1→H2→H3). Nav in . Main content in . Footer in

Images: All in WebP format. Responsive srcset for 3 breakpoints (mobile/tablet/desktop). Lazy loading on all below-fold images.
Fonts: Google Fonts loaded asynchronously with font-display: swap. Only 2–3 font weights per typeface loaded.
